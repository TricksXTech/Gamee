Magnet Sort

Guide every metal piece to its matching goal.

Level: 1
Moves: 0
Pieces left: 0
Red piece / goal Blue piece / goal Obstacle
How to play: Tap a magnet to switch its polarity. Then tap a neighboring metal piece. A magnet attracts a piece of the same color and pushes away a piece of the opposite color. A piece moves one square if the destination is empty. Place every piece on its matching goal.
